Smart Civic AI is a lightweight community issue management platform designed in alignment with UN SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities and Communities). It simplifies reporting local infrastructure problems (e.g. damaged utilities, sanitation tasks) by using a smart routing mechanism to triage and forward incidents directly to the correct operations teams.
When a ticket is submitted, our system analyzes the category, description keywords, and location data. In production, an AI model (like Gemini) evaluates severity levels (assigning Low, Medium, or High priority ratings) and maps the case to the appropriate operations team. The local storage fallback replicates this behavior.
SDG 11 is one of 17 UN Sustainable Development Goals, targeting the creation of inclusive, safe, resilient, and sustainable settlements. This platform supports these targets by accelerating response times to local waste accumulation, utility outages, and infrastructure issues.
