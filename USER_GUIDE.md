# User Guide

Welcome to the **Smart Civic AI** platform. This guide details how to navigate the platform, submit an issue ticket, retrieve GPS coordinates, and track progress timelines.

---

## 🧭 Navigation Overview

The platform features a sticky top navigation menu on every page:
* **Home**: Landing page with platform highlights, UN SDG 11 metrics, and quick action buttons.
* **Submit Ticket**: Submit new community issues.
* **Track Ticket**: Search and view the status of active tickets.
* **Workflow**: Detailed overview of the AI routing architecture.
* **FAQ**: Collapsible accordion answering common questions.
* **Data Security Policy (Footer)**: Details on local cache storage and GPS permissions.

---

## 📝 Submitting a Ticket (Step-by-Step)

To report an issue:

1. Click on **Submit Ticket** in the navigation bar or the black **"Submit Issue Ticket"** button in the home page hero section.
2. Complete the required form fields:
   * **Issue Title**: Provide a brief title (e.g., *"Water leak at Main St Intersection"*). Minimum 5 characters.
   * **Category / Issue Type**: Select the closest category from the dropdown (Waste Management, Infrastructure Group, Water Services, Road Maintenance, Public Safety, or Others).
   * **Detailed Description**: Describe the issue (e.g., specific hazard level, dimensions, duration). Minimum 15 characters.
   * **Location / Landmark**: Provide a written address or nearby landmark (e.g., *"Opposite the Tech Park Central gate"*).
3. Add **GPS Coordinates** (Optional but recommended):
   * Click the **"Get Location"** button.
   * Your browser will prompt you to share location access. Click **"Allow"**.
   * The latitude and longitude fields will populate automatically.
4. Upload an **Evidence Image** (Optional):
   * Drag an image file (PNG, JPG, JPEG) and drop it onto the dotted dropzone box, or click **"browse"** to select an image from your device.
   * A preview thumbnail will render below the box. Images must be under 2MB.
5. Click **"Submit Ticket"**.
6. On successful submission, you will be redirected to the **Success Receipt** page.

---

## 📄 Understanding the Success Receipt

After submitting a ticket, you will see a success confirmation receipt showing the assigned case metadata:
- **Reference ID**: A unique identifier generated by the system (e.g., `SCAI-2026-9081`). **Save this ID to track your ticket status later.**
- **AI Severity Rating**: High, Medium, or Low (automatically assigned based on issue description and safety risks).
- **Assigned Operations Team**: The team routed to manage the task (e.g., *Infrastructure Group*).
- **AI Analysis Summary**: Explains the triage analysis completed by the AI engine.
- **Print Receipt**: Click this to print or save a PDF copy of your confirmation.

## 🔍 Tracking a Ticket (Step-by-Step)

To check the progress of an active ticket:

1. Click **Track Ticket** in the navigation bar.
2. Enter your unique reference code (e.g., `SCAI-2026-9081`) into the search box.
3. Click **"Search"**.
4. The page will display the **Resolution Timeline** showing the status of your issue:
   - **Step 1: Submitted**: Confirms the ticket is registered.
   - **Step 2: AI Analysis**: AI has parsed and prioritized the report.
   - **Step 3: Assigned**: Ticket routed to the designated operations team.
   - **Step 4: Processing**: Field operations team scheduled or dispatched.
   - **Step 5: Resolved**: Maintenance completed.
5. The details block below the timeline will render all associated data, including the uploaded image and coordinates.
